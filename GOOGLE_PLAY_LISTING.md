# BharatPlay Google Play Listing

## App name

BharatPlay

## Short description

Watch, discover, upload and share videos with the BharatPlay community.

## Full description

BharatPlay is a video and creator platform built for discovering entertainment, following channels, and sharing your own voice.

Explore a growing mix of short videos and long-form content across entertainment, music, gaming, technology, education, sports, lifestyle, and more. Follow channels you enjoy, react to videos, join conversations, and keep your watch experience organized.

### What you can do on BharatPlay

- Discover short videos and long-form entertainment.
- Follow creators and channels that match your interests.
- Upload videos, create a channel, and share original content.
- Like, comment, and participate in a respectful community.
- Continue watching videos and manage your watch history.
- Use reporting and safety tools to help keep the platform welcoming.
- Manage your profile, account settings, privacy choices, and data deletion requests.

### Advertising and rewards

BharatPlay may show advertising, including optional rewarded advertising where available. Ads and mediation partners can vary by country, app version, and release configuration. Rewarded experiences do not guarantee cash payment. Any points or promotional rewards are subject to eligibility, fraud prevention, program rules, and the availability of an approved BharatPlay payout program.

Do not click or interact with ads only to manipulate rewards. BharatPlay may limit, reverse, freeze, or remove rewards connected to invalid activity, automation, duplicate accounts, fraud, or policy violations.

### Your privacy and control

We explain our data practices in the BharatPlay Privacy Policy. You can review the Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, Community Guidelines, Content Policy, Copyright Policy, Help and Support information, and Account & Data Deletion instructions from the BharatPlay website and app policy center.

BharatPlay is designed for users aged 13 and above, or the minimum age required in the user's jurisdiction. Users under 18 should use the service with parent or guardian consent.

## Release checklist

Before publishing a release, confirm the following values in Google Play Console and the app build:

- Privacy Policy URL is public and matches the released data practices.
- Data Safety form includes account data, uploaded content, usage data, device identifiers, analytics, advertising, and deletion behavior as applicable.
- Advertising ID declaration is completed if the released build or ad SDK accesses the Android advertising ID.
- AppLovin MAX, Unity Ads, LevelPlay, Mintegral, and any other mediation SDKs are listed only when actually included in the build.
- Test ad unit IDs are removed or replaced with the correct production IDs before release.
- Rewarded-ad callbacks are verified server-side before any points are granted.
- Real payout methods, thresholds, conversion rates, and support contacts are configured before enabling withdrawals.
